# C722 Glossary Flashcards

## Card 1
**Front:** What is a project?  
**Back:** A temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result.

## Card 2
**Front:** What is a program?  
**Back:** A group of related projects managed together to achieve benefits not available individually.

## Card 3
**Front:** What is a portfolio?  
**Back:** A collection of projects and programs managed together to achieve strategic objectives.

## Card 4
**Front:** Define the triple constraint.  
**Back:** Scope, time, and cost — changing one affects the others.

## Card 5
**Front:** What is a work breakdown structure (WBS)?  
**Back:** A hierarchical decomposition of project deliverables into smaller work packages.

## Card 6
**Front:** What is a critical path?  
**Back:** The longest sequence of activities that determines the minimum project duration.

## Card 7
**Front:** What is float/slack?  
**Back:** The amount of time an activity can be delayed without affecting the project end date.

## Card 8
**Front:** What is a risk?  
**Back:** An uncertain event that may positively or negatively impact project objectives.

## Card 9
**Front:** What is an issue?  
**Back:** A problem that has already occurred and requires immediate action.

## Card 10
**Front:** What is quality assurance?  
**Back:** Process-focused activities that ensure quality standards are built into the project.

## Card 11
**Front:** What is quality control?  
**Back:** Product-focused activities that verify deliverables meet quality requirements.

## Card 12
**Front:** What is a change request?  
**Back:** A formal proposal to modify scope, schedule, cost, or deliverables.

## Card 13
**Front:** What is NPV (Net Present Value)?  
**Back:** The present value of future cash flows minus initial investment; higher NPV is preferred.

## Card 14
**Front:** What is IRR (Internal Rate of Return)?  
**Back:** The discount rate that makes NPV equal zero; higher IRR is preferred.

## Card 15
**Front:** What is payback period?  
**Back:** The time required to recover the initial investment.

## Card 16
**Front:** What is opportunity cost?  
**Back:** The value of the next best alternative that is not chosen.

## Card 17
**Front:** What is parametric estimating?  
**Back:** Uses statistical relationships (e.g., cost per unit) to estimate cost or duration.

## Card 18
**Front:** What is bottom-up estimating?  
**Back:** Estimates each work package individually and sums them for total cost/duration.

## Card 19
**Front:** What is top-down estimating?  
**Back:** Uses historical data or expert judgment to estimate the entire project.

## Card 20
**Front:** What is a project charter?  
**Back:** A document that formally authorizes the project and gives the PM authority.
