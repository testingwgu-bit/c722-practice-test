# C722 Glossary Flashcards

## Card 1
**Front:** What is a project?  
**Back:** A temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 2
**Front:** What is a program?  
**Back:** A group of related projects managed together to achieve benefits not available individually.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 3
**Front:** What is a portfolio?  
**Back:** A collection of projects and programs managed together to achieve strategic objectives.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 4
**Front:** Define the triple constraint.  
**Back:** Scope, time, and cost — changing one affects the others.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 5
**Front:** What is a work breakdown structure (WBS)?  
**Back:** A hierarchical decomposition of project deliverables into smaller work packages.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 6
**Front:** What is a critical path?  
**Back:** The longest sequence of activities that determines the minimum project duration.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 7
**Front:** What is float/slack?  
**Back:** The amount of time an activity can be delayed without affecting the project end date.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 8
**Front:** What is a risk?  
**Back:** An uncertain event that may positively or negatively impact project objectives.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 9
**Front:** What is an issue?  
**Back:** A problem that has already occurred and requires immediate action.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 10
**Front:** What is quality assurance?  
**Back:** Process-focused activities that ensure quality standards are built into the project.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 11
**Front:** What is quality control?  
**Back:** Product-focused activities that verify deliverables meet quality requirements.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 12
**Front:** What is a change request?  
**Back:** A formal proposal to modify scope, schedule, cost, or deliverables.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 13
**Front:** What is NPV (Net Present Value)?  
**Back:** The present value of future cash flows minus initial investment; higher NPV is preferred.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 14
**Front:** What is IRR (Internal Rate of Return)?  
**Back:** The discount rate that makes NPV equal zero; higher IRR is preferred.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 15
**Front:** What is payback period?  
**Back:** The time required to recover the initial investment.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 16
**Front:** What is opportunity cost?  
**Back:** The value of the next best alternative that is not chosen.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 17
**Front:** What is parametric estimating?  
**Back:** Uses statistical relationships (e.g., cost per unit) to estimate cost or duration.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 18
**Front:** What is bottom-up estimating?  
**Back:** Estimates each work package individually and sums them for total cost/duration.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 19
**Front:** What is top-down estimating?  
**Back:** Uses historical data or expert judgment to estimate the entire project.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 20
**Front:** What is a project charter?  
**Back:** A document that formally authorizes the project and gives the PM authority.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 21  
**Front:** What is scope creep?  
**Back:** Uncontrolled expansion of project scope without adjustments to time, cost, or resources.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 22  
**Front:** What is a baseline?  
**Back:** The approved version of scope, schedule, or cost used to measure performance.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 23  
**Front:** What is a stakeholder?  
**Back:** Anyone who has interest or influence in the project.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 24  
**Front:** What is a deliverable?  
**Back:** A measurable, tangible output produced as part of the project.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 25  
**Front:** What is a dependency?  
**Back:** A relationship where one task relies on another to start or finish.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 26  
**Front:** Define Finish-to-Start (FS).  
**Back:** The successor cannot start until the predecessor finishes.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 27  
**Front:** Define Start-to-Start (SS).  
**Back:** The successor cannot start until the predecessor starts.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 28  
Front: Define Finish-to-Finish (FF).  
**Back:** The successor cannot finish until the predecessor finishes.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 29  
**Front:** Define Start-to-Finish (SF).  
**Back:** The successor cannot finish until the predecessor starts.
**Tags:** #low-priority

## Card 30  
**Front:** What is a milestone?  
**Back:** A significant event or checkpoint with zero duration.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 31  
**Front:** What is a project sponsor?  
**Back:** The person who provides financial resources and approves major decisions.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 32  
**Front:** What is a PMO?  
**Back:** A department that standardizes project processes and supports project managers.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 33  
**Front:** What is risk appetite?  
**Back:** The amount of uncertainty an organization is willing to accept.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 34  
**Front:** What is risk tolerance?  
**Back:** The acceptable range of variation in project outcomes.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 35  
**Front:** What is a risk threshold?  
**Back:** The point at which risk becomes unacceptable.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 36  
**Front:** What is a risk register?  
**Back:** A document listing identified risks, analysis, and planned responses.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 37  
**Front:** What is a change log?  
**Back:** A record of all change requests and their status.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 38  
**Front:** What is a functional organization?  
**Back:** Structure where employees are grouped by department; PM has little authority.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 39  
**Front:** What is a projectized organization?  
**Back:** Structure where the PM has full authority and teams are dedicated.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 40  
**Front:** What is a matrix organization?  
**Back:** Structure where employees report to both functional and project managers.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 41  
**Front:** What is crashing?  
**Back:** Adding resources to shorten the schedule at increased cost.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 42  
**Front:** What is fast-tracking?  
**Back:** Overlapping tasks to shorten the schedule, increasing risk.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 43  
**Front:** What is resource leveling?  
**Back:** Adjusting the schedule to reduce resource overload; may extend the timeline.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 44  
**Front:** What is resource smoothing?  
**Back:** Adjusting non-critical tasks to reduce overload without affecting the critical path.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 45  
**Front:** What is a Gantt chart?  
**Back:** A visual timeline showing tasks, durations, and dependencies.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 46  
**Front:** What is a network diagram?  
**Back:** A visual representation of task sequencing and dependencies.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 47  
**Front:** What is Earned Value (EV)?  
**Back:** The value of work completed to date.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 48  
**Front:** What is Planned Value (PV)?  
**Back:** The value of work scheduled to be completed by a certain date.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 49  
**Front:** What is Actual Cost (AC)?  
**Back:** The cost incurred for work performed.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 50  
**Front:** What is Cost Variance (CV)?  
**Back:** EV – AC; positive means under budget.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 51  
**Front:** What is Schedule Variance (SV)?  
**Back:** EV – PV; positive means ahead of schedule.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 52  
**Front:** What is CPI?  
**Back:** EV ÷ AC; measures cost efficiency.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 53  
**Front:** What is SPI?  
**Back:** EV ÷ PV; measures schedule efficiency.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 54  
**Front:** What is EAC?  
**Back:** Estimate at Completion; projected total cost of the project.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 55  
**Front:** What is ETC?  
**Back:** Estimate to Complete; cost needed to finish remaining work.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 56  
**Front:** What is TCPI?  
**Back:** To Complete Performance Index; efficiency needed to meet the budget.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 57  
**Front:** What is a work package?  
**Back:** The lowest-level deliverable in the WBS.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 58  
**Front:** What is a project charter?  
**Back:** Document that authorizes the project and gives the PM authority.
**Tags:** #must-know

## Card 59  
**Front:** What is a feasibility study?  
**Back:** Analysis determining whether a project is viable.
**Tags:** #medium-priority

## Card 60  
**Front:** What is a lessons learned document?  
**Back:** A record of what went well, what didn’t, and recommendations for future projects.
**Tags:** #medium-priority
