# C722 Concept Flashcards

## Card 1
**Front:** In which phase of the project life cycle is the project charter developed?  
**Back:** The Defining/Initiating phase.

## Card 2
**Front:** When would an organization use Net Present Value (NPV) in project selection?  
**Back:** When comparing projects based on the present value of future cash flows; higher NPV is preferred.

## Card 3
**Front:** What is the main drawback of checklist project selection?  
**Back:** It is subjective and biased; it does not quantify benefits or costs.

## Card 4
**Front:** A project has O = 4, M = 6, P = 10. What is the three‑point estimate?  
**Back:** (4 + 6 + 10) / 3 = 20 / 3 ≈ 6.67 days.

## Card 5
**Front:** What does a CPI of 0.80 indicate?  
**Back:** The project is over budget; it is earning only 80 cents of value per dollar spent.

## Card 6
**Front:** What does an SPI of 1.20 indicate?  
**Back:** The project is ahead of schedule.

## Card 7
**Front:** What is the critical path?  
**Back:** The longest path through the project network; determines minimum project duration.

## Card 8
**Front:** What is the difference between quality assurance and quality control?  
**Back:** QA is process‑focused; QC is product‑focused.

## Card 9
**Front:** What is the correct risk response for a high‑impact, high‑probability threat?  
**Back:** Avoid or mitigate.

## Card 10
**Front:** What should a PM do if a stakeholder requests a major scope change mid‑project?  
**Back:** Submit a formal change request and perform impact analysis before approval.

## Card 11
**Front:** What is the purpose of a WBS?  
**Back:** To break down deliverables into manageable work packages.

## Card 12
**Front:** What is the main advantage of bottom‑up estimating?  
**Back:** It is more accurate because it estimates each work package individually.

## Card 13
**Front:** What is the main advantage of top‑down estimating?  
**Back:** It is fast and useful early in the project when details are limited.

## Card 14
**Front:** What is the purpose of lessons learned?  
**Back:** To capture what worked and what didn’t for future projects.

## Card 15
**Front:** What is the difference between a risk and an issue?  
**Back:** A risk is uncertain; an issue has already occurred.

## Card 16
**Front:** What happens to cost and schedule when scope increases?  
**Back:** Both cost and schedule must increase.

## Card 17
**Front:** Which project life cycle phases are iterative?  
**Back:** Planning and Executing.

## Card 18
**Front:** What is the primary purpose of a WBS?  
**Back:** To define and structure project scope.

## Card 19
**Front:** What does CPI < 1 indicate?  
**Back:** The project is over budget.

## Card 20
**Front:** What does SPI < 1 indicate?  
**Back:** The project is behind schedule.

## Card 21
**Front:** What is the longest path through a project schedule called?  
**Back:** The critical path.

## Card 22
**Front:** What risk response reduces probability or impact?  
**Back:** Mitigation.

## Card 23
**Front:** What risk response shifts ownership of the risk?  
**Back:** Transfer.

## Card 24
**Front:** What risk response eliminates the threat entirely?  
**Back:** Avoidance.

## Card 25
**Front:** What risk response accepts the consequences without action?  
**Back:** Acceptance.

## Card 26
**Front:** What schedule compression technique overlaps tasks?  
**Back:** Fast‑tracking.

## Card 27
**Front:** What schedule compression technique adds resources?  
**Back:** Crashing.

## Card 28
**Front:** What organizational structure has dual reporting lines?  
**Back:** Matrix.

## Card 29
**Front:** What organizational structure gives the PM full authority?  
**Back:** Projectized.

## Card 30
**Front:** What organizational structure is department‑based?  
**Back:** Functional.

## Card 31
**Front:** What is the formula for Cost Variance (CV)?  
**Back:** CV = EV – AC.

## Card 32
**Front:** What is the formula for Schedule Variance (SV)?  
**Back:** SV = EV – PV.

## Card 33
**Front:** What is the formula for CPI?  
**Back:** CPI = EV ÷ AC.

## Card 34
**Front:** What is the formula for SPI?  
**Back:** SPI = EV ÷ PV.

## Card 35
**Front:** What is the formula for EAC using CPI?  
**Back:** EAC = Budget ÷ CPI.

## Card 36
**Front:** What is the formula for ETC?  
**Back:** ETC = EAC – AC.

## Card 37
**Front:** What is the formula for TCPI?  
**Back:** (Budget – EV) ÷ (Budget – AC).

## Card 38
**Front:** What is the triangular estimate formula?  
**Back:** (O + M + P) ÷ 3.

## Card 39
**Front:** What is the beta estimate formula?  
**Back:** (O + 4M + P) ÷ 6.

## Card 40
**Front:** What is the standard deviation formula?  
**Back:** (P – O) ÷ 6.
