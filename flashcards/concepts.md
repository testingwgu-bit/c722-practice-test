# C722 Concept Flashcards

## Card 1
**Front:** In which phase of the project life cycle is the project charter developed?  
**Back:** The Defining/Initiating phase.

## Card 2
**Front:** When would an organization use Net Present Value (NPV) in project selection?  
**Back:** When comparing projects based on the present value of future cash flows; higher NPV is preferred.

## Card 3
**Front:** What is the main drawback of checklist project selection?  
**Back:** It is subjective and biased; it does not quantify benefits or costs.

## Card 4
**Front:** A project has O = 4, M = 6, P = 10. What is the three‑point estimate?  
**Back:** (4 + 6 + 10) / 3 = 20 / 3 ≈ 6.67 days.

## Card 5
**Front:** What does a CPI of 0.80 indicate?  
**Back:** The project is over budget; it is earning only 80 cents of value per dollar spent.

## Card 6
**Front:** What does an SPI of 1.20 indicate?  
**Back:** The project is ahead of schedule.

## Card 7
**Front:** What is the critical path?  
**Back:** The longest path through the project network; determines minimum project duration.

## Card 8
**Front:** What is the difference between quality assurance and quality control?  
**Back:** QA is process‑focused; QC is product‑focused.

## Card 9
**Front:** What is the correct risk response for a high‑impact, high‑probability threat?  
**Back:** Avoid or mitigate.

## Card 10
**Front:** What should a PM do if a stakeholder requests a major scope change mid‑project?  
**Back:** Submit a formal change request and perform impact analysis before approval.

## Card 11
**Front:** What is the purpose of a WBS?  
**Back:** To break down deliverables into manageable work packages.

## Card 12
**Front:** What is the main advantage of bottom‑up estimating?  
**Back:** It is more accurate because it estimates each work package individually.

## Card 13
**Front:** What is the main advantage of top‑down estimating?  
**Back:** It is fast and useful early in the project when details are limited.

## Card 14
**Front:** What is the purpose of lessons learned?  
**Back:** To capture what worked and what didn’t for future projects.

## Card 15
**Front:** What is the difference between a risk and an issue?  
**Back:** A risk is uncertain; an issue has already occurred.
