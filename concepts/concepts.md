# C722 Project Management — Core Concepts

A complete, exam‑aligned concept reference based on the C722 OA topics.

# Priority Tags

- #medium-priority → Important but less frequent

## 1. Project, Program, Portfolio #must-know
- **Project:** Temporary effort that creates a unique result.  
- **Program:** Group of related projects managed together.  
- **Portfolio:** Group of programs aligned to strategic goals.

---

## 2. Project Life Cycle Phases #must-know
### **Defining**
### Defining #must-know
- Clarify purpose and objectives  
- Create project charter  
- Decide whether to proceed  
- Ends when charter is approved  

### **Planning** #must-know
- Detailed planning  
- Create WBS  
- Establish baselines (scope, schedule, cost)  
- Ends when baselines are approved  

### **Executing** #must-know
- Work performed  
- Deliverables created  
- Monitor progress  
- Watch for scope creep  
- May return to planning if changes occur  

### **Closing** #must-know
- Deliver outcomes  
- Release resources  
- Close procurement  
- Document lessons learned  

### **Iterative Phases** #medium-priority
- **Planning** and **Executing**

---

## 3. Triple Constraint (Scope, Cost, Time) #must-know
- Changing one affects the others  
- **Scope:** Features, deliverables  
- **Cost:** Budget, resources  
- **Time:** Schedule, deadlines  

---

## 4. Change Management Process #must-know
1. Identify change  
2. Evaluate change  
3. Decision  
4. Communicate change  

---

## 5. Quality Management #must-know
### **Quality Management** #must-know
- Define requirements and how they will be measured  

### **Quality Assurance** #medium-priority
- Validate that processes and measurements are appropriate  

### **Quality Control** #must-know
- Inspect deliverables  
- Ensure work meets requirements  
- Correct defects  

---

## 6. Risk Management #must-know
1. Identify risks  
2. Analyze risks (qualitative + quantitative)  
3. Plan responses  

### **Risk Appetite** #medium-priority
- How much uncertainty the organization is willing to accept  

### **Risk Tolerance** #medium-priority
- How much variation in outcomes is acceptable  

### **Risk Threshold** #medium-priority
- Point at which risk becomes unacceptable  

### **Risk Responses** #must-know
- **Avoid:** Eliminate the threat  
- **Mitigate:** Reduce probability/impact  
- **Transfer:** Insurance, outsourcing  
- **Accept:** No action taken  

---

## 7. Organizational Structures #must-know
### **Functional** #medium-priority
- Department‑based  
- PM has little authority  

### **Projectized** #must-know
- PM has full authority  
- Dedicated teams  

### **Matrix** #must-know
- Dual reporting (functional + PM)  
- Shared resources  

---

## 8. PMO (Project Management Office) #must-know
- Aligns projects with strategy  
- Monitors resource availability  
- Reduces overhead  
- Maintains policies, procedures, lessons learned  
- Ensures consistency across projects  

---

## 9. Project Selection Methods #medium-priority
### **Non‑Numeric** #medium-priority
- **Competitive Necessity:** Needed to stay competitive  
- **Operating Necessity:** Needed to maintain operations  
- **Sacred Cow:** Suggested by senior leadership  

### **Numeric** #medium-priority
- **Payback Period:** Cost ÷ monthly return  
- **NPV:** Present value of future cash flows  
- **IRR:** Rate of return on project  
- **Scoring Models:** Weighted criteria  

---

## 10. Cost Estimation Methods #must-know
### **Top‑Down** #medium-priority
- Based on past projects  
- Used early (defining phase)  

### **Bottom‑Up** #must-know
- Estimate each work package  
- Most accurate  

### **Parametric** #must-know
- **Ratio:** Cost per unit × number of units  
- **Apportion:** Allocate budget by percentage to major work areas  

---

## 11. Duration Estimation Methods #must-know
### **One‑Point** #medium-priority
- Single expert estimate  

### **Three‑Point (Triangular)** #must-know
- (O + M + P) / 3  

### **Beta (Weighted Average)** #must-know
- (O + 4M + P) / 6  

### **Standard Deviation** #must-know
- (P – O) / 6  

---

## 12. Schedule Compression #must-know
### **Fast‑Tracking** #must-know
- Overlap tasks  
- Increases risk  

### **Crashing** #must-know
- Add resources  
- Increases cost  

---

## 13. Critical Path #must-know
- Longest path through the network  
- Determines minimum project duration  
- Tasks on critical path have **zero float**  

### **Forward Pass**
- Earliest start/finish dates  

### **Backward Pass**
- Latest start/finish dates  

---

## 14. Gantt Charts #must-know
- Visual timeline  
- Shows dependencies  
- Shows overlapping tasks  
- Used to determine correct sequence  

---

## 15. Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) #must-know
- Hierarchical decomposition  
- Work packages follow the **8/80 rule**  
- Defines scope  
- Does NOT show schedule  

---

## 16. Earned Value Management (EVM) #must-know
### **Formulas**
- **EV:** % complete × budget  
- **CV:** EV – AC  
- **SV:** EV – PV  
- **CPI:** EV ÷ AC  
- **SPI:** EV ÷ PV  
- **EAC:** Budget ÷ CPI  
- **ETC:** EAC – AC  
- **TCPI:** (Budget – EV) ÷ (Budget – AC)  
- **Percent Complete:** EV ÷ Budget  

### **Interpretation**
- CPI < 1 → Over budget  
- SPI < 1 → Behind schedule
